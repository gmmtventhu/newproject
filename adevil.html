About Us

We are Devicate

Devicate is a small collective of hands and eyes. We build thresholds — pages that feel less like screens and more like doors left ajar in a house you don't remember renting.

We started with three rules and one lantern. The rules were: nothing should feel finished, nothing should feel safe, everything should still be beautiful enough to stay for. The lantern was for the fourth rule, which we keep off the record.

悪魔の瞳 is our first seal. It was drawn from the inside. The eye is real in the only way that matters — it notices you, and it does not forget the ones who tap.

We are quiet. We answer. We do not explain the fourth rule.

House Creed

  1. Leave the light low; the eye prefers it.
  2. Never blink first.
  3. Beauty is a trap, so make it a good one.
  4. If it speaks, answer politely.
  5. Close the seal behind you.
